# Sushi Business Plan

## Executive Summary
As a student, I’ve always been passionate about sushi—a beautiful blend of art, cuisine, and culture. My vision is to create a sushi food truck called 'Rolling Fresh' that offers traditional Japanese sushi, fusion options, and a selection of vegetarian items. The idea is to make sushi accessible and affordable for everyone, especially students who are always on the go.

## Business Overview
### Business Name:
Rolling Fresh  
### Business Structure:
Limited Liability Company (LLC)  
### Location:
University campuses, local festivals, and busy urban areas.  
### Hours of Operation:
11am - 9pm, Tuesday through Sunday.

## Market Analysis
### Industry Overview:
The sushi market is growing, especially among younger demographics. Food trucks have gained massive popularity due to their mobility and lower start-up costs compared to traditional restaurants.

### Target Market:
Our main target customers are:
- University students looking for quick, healthy, and unique meal options.
- Young professionals seeking a fresh lunch alternative.
- Families at local events or parks looking for fun and tasty food.

### Competition:
We’ll face competition from other food trucks and casual dining restaurants, but our emphasis on quality, fresh ingredients, and unique menu offerings will help us stand out.

## Marketing Strategy
### Branding:
Our branding will reflect the freshness and artistry of sushi, with a logo that incorporates Japanese elements and modern design. 

### Promotion:
- Social media campaigns targeting local student populations.
- Collaborations with university organizations for events.
- Loyalty discounts for recurring customers.

## Operations Plan
- **Supply Chain**: Partner with local seafood suppliers to ensure the freshest ingredients.
- **Employee Training**: Hire experienced sushi chefs and staff for optimal customer service.
- **Food Safety**: Adhere to strict food safety regulations and maintain high hygiene standards.

## Financial Plan
### Start-Up Costs:
- Food truck purchase and customization: $50,000  
- Initial inventory of ingredients: $5,000  
- Licensing and permits: $2,000  
- Marketing and promotions: $3,000  
- Miscellaneous: $5,000  
### Funding and Revenue:
I plan to fund the business through a combination of personal savings, small business loans, and potential crowdfunding campaigns. Our revenue will primarily come from food sales.

### Financial Projections:
Expecting to break even within the first year, with a projected revenue of $150,000 in the first 12 months, growing as we expand our reach and customer base.

## Conclusion
Rolling Fresh aims to be more than just another sushi truck; we want to create a community around fresh food and cultural appreciation, all while providing a fun, casual dining experience. With the right strategies and passion, we’re excited to make our mark in the sushi world!
